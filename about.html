✧ About The Masterpiece ✧

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Following the defeat of the Demon King, the elven mage Frieren and her party bring peace to the realm before parting. As an immortal elf, fifty years pass for her in the blink of an eye, forcing her to witness her former companions pass away. Stricken with regret for not knowing them better, she initiates a brand new quest to understand the hearts of mortals.
